Discovery questions by level

Key questions for each descent level

L1 — surface friction
  • What's difficult today?
  • Where does the process break?
  • What feels manual?
L2 — operational impact
  • How often does that happen?
  • Who's impacted?
  • What does that slow down?
  • What's the downstream effect?
  • How much manual work does that create?
  • What happens if that doesn't improve?
L3 — executive metric
  • What's the cost of that delay?
  • How does leadership measure success here?
  • What KPI is impacted most?
  • What happens if this doesn't improve?
  • What would faster alignment unlock?
  • What's the risk of continuing this way?
  • How much engineering time gets lost?
The real skill isn't asking more questions. It's staying on the problem long enough to ask the right ones. When you hear "we duplicate work" — don't demo. Ask how often, between which teams, what the cost is, and what metric suffers. That's the descent.
